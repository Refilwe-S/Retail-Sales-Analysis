#  Retail Sales Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of retail sales data to uncover key business insights that support revenue growth, product optimization, and strategic decision-making.

The analysis focuses on identifying trends in sales performance, customer behavior, and product category performance using data analytics and visualization tools.

---

##  Objectives

* Analyze overall revenue and sales trends over time
* Identify top-performing product categories
* Understand customer purchasing patterns
* Evaluate performance across different customer segments
* Provide data-driven recommendations for business growth

---

##  Dataset Description

The dataset contains historical transactional data, including:

* Transaction Date
* Product Category
* Quantity Sold
* Total Amount (Revenue)
* Customer Gender
* Sales-related attributes for performance analysis

---

##  Tools & Technologies

* **SQL** – Data cleaning, transformation, and querying
* **Excel / Google Sheets** – Data preparation and pivot analysis
* **Looker Studio** – Dashboard creation and data visualization

##  Key Insights

* Revenue trends highlight peak sales periods and seasonal patterns
* Certain product categories consistently outperform others
* Customer segments show varying purchasing behaviors
* Opportunities exist to improve underperforming areas

---

##  Recommendations

* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products
* Introduce promotions during low-sales periods
* Optimize inventory based on demand trends
* Use customer segmentation for targeted marketing strategies
